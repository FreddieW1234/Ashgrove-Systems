What I build

One store. One system.
No spreadsheets in between.

Most Shopify agencies stop at the storefront. The real work starts when the order lands in your office. I build both halves and make them talk.

01

Shopify storefront

A fast, well-built store that fits how you actually sell: personalised products, quantity breaks, quote requests, trade accounts and the admin set-up to run it.

  • Theme build
  • Custom apps

Staff portal

Shipping & labels

Case study

Bite Promotions & BiteBoard

Bite Promotions sells promotional merchandise: printed, branded, personalised products with artwork to approve on every job. I built their Shopify site at bitepromotions.co.uk and BiteBoard, the internal system their office runs on every day.

  1. 01 Order comes in on Shopify
  2. 02 Artwork and proofs handled in BiteBoard
  3. 03 Production tracked job by job
  4. 04 FedEx label printed on the office Zebra
  5. 05 Klaviyo order updates and “it’s on its way” email, automatically

No re-keying at any step.

10+ hours a week of admin handed back to the office
1 view of every job, so staff and customers always know where it is

Before BiteBoard, every order meant the same details typed out three or four times: the job sheet, the courier site, the email to the customer. Now it comes in from the website and we just do the work. If something goes wrong, Freddie fixes it the same day.

Tony, Owner, Bite Promotions

About

One person. I write the code myself.

I’m Freddie Wadley and Ashgrove Systems is just me, based between Coventry and Swansea. There is no account manager and no outsourced dev team. The person you meet at the process review is the person who designs the system, writes the code and answers the phone when something changes.

I’m studying Accounting & Finance at the University of Warwick, which is why the systems I build care about the numbers as much as the screens: invoices that add up, trade prices that are right, stock that matches.
