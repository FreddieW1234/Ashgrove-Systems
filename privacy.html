Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ashgrove Systems collects, why, and what you can do about it. If anything is unclear, email me and I'll explain.

Who I am

Ashgrove Systems is run by Freddie Wadley, based in Coventry and Swansea. I'm responsible for any personal information described here, which makes me the "controller" under UK data protection law.

Contact: freddie@ashgrovesystems.co.uk or 07555 142782.

This website

This website has no contact forms, sets no cookies, and uses no analytics or tracking. It doesn't store anything in your browser.

Like every website, it relies on a few services that receive your device's IP address and basic browser details when they deliver files to you:

  • Render hosts the site and keeps standard server logs for security and troubleshooting.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and unpkg supply the code libraries that run the animations.

I don't receive or use that information myself. Each service handles it under its own privacy policy.

When you contact me

If you email or phone me, I'll have your name, contact details and whatever you tell me about your business. I use it to reply, arrange a process review and, if we work together, to run the project.

My lawful basis is legitimate interests: responding to an enquiry you started. If we go on to agree work, it becomes contract.

Business contacts I reach out to

I sometimes contact businesses that might benefit from my work. I use business contact details that the business publishes itself, usually on its own website.

I keep a simple record of who I've contacted and when, so I don't contact anyone twice by mistake. My lawful basis is legitimate interests: telling businesses about a relevant service. Every email tells you how to opt out, and if you reply "no thanks" I'll add you to a do-not-contact list and won't email again.

Client projects

When I build or support systems for a client, I may need access to their Shopify store and connected services, which contain their customers' details. In that work I act as a processor: I only use that information to do the job the client has asked for, I don't copy it anywhere it doesn't need to be, and I remove my access when the work ends. The client remains responsible for their own customers' data and privacy notice.

Who else sees your information

I don't sell personal information or share it for anyone else's marketing. I use Microsoft 365 for email, calendar and file storage.

Microsoft may store data outside the UK. Where it does, it uses the safeguards UK law requires, such as approved contract terms.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go anywhere: up to 2 years after we last spoke.
  • Outreach records: up to 2 years after I last contacted you. Do-not-contact requests are kept for as long as I'm in business, so I can honour them.
  • Client records and invoices: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • tell you what information I hold about you and give you a copy
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information, where I don't need to keep it by law
  • stop using it, including for any marketing, at any time

Just email freddie@ashgrovesystems.co.uk. There's no charge, and I'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If I change how I use personal information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
