Terms of business

Last updated 16 November 2026

These terms apply to all work Ashgrove Systems does for you. "I", "me" and "Ashgrove Systems" mean Freddie Wadley, trading as Ashgrove Systems. "You" means the business I'm working for.

1. The process review

  1. The process review is free and you're under no obligation to hire me afterwards.
  2. Afterwards I'll send you a written plan and, if you'd like to go ahead, a proposal.

2. Proposals and phases

  1. Work is split into phases. Each phase has a written proposal setting out what I'll build (the "scope"), the fixed price and an estimated delivery date.
  2. A proposal is valid for 30 days from the date on it.
  3. Work starts once you've accepted the proposal and these terms in writing (email is fine), and paid the deposit.
  4. The fixed price covers everything in the scope. Anything you ask for that isn't in the scope is a change (see section 4).

3. Payment

  1. Deposit: 50% of the phase price, paid before work starts.
  2. Balance: the remaining 50%, invoiced when the phase is delivered.
  3. Invoices are due within 14 days.
  4. If an invoice is overdue, I may pause work until it's paid. I may also charge interest and compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.
  5. Prices are in pounds sterling. I'm not currently VAT registered, so no VAT is charged.

4. Changes

  1. If you'd like something that isn't in the agreed scope, I'll tell you the extra cost and any effect on the delivery date before doing it.
  2. I'll only do the extra work once you've agreed in writing.

5. What I need from you

  1. Access to the accounts and systems the work needs, such as Shopify, Klaviyo and courier accounts, set up so I use my own login rather than yours.
  2. Content, information and decisions when I ask for them.
  3. Feedback and approvals within 5 working days.

If these take longer, the delivery date moves back by the same amount of time. It's not a breach of these terms on either side.

6. Delivery and checking the work

  1. When a phase is delivered, you have 10 working days to check it and tell me about anything that doesn't work as described in the scope.
  2. I'll fix those problems at no extra cost.
  3. If I don't hear from you within 10 working days, or you start using the work in your business, the phase counts as accepted.
  4. For 30 days after acceptance, I'll also fix any bugs in the work I delivered at no extra cost. After that, fixes are covered by a support plan or charged separately.

7. Monthly support

  1. Monthly support is optional. What it covers, and the monthly fee, are set out in the support proposal.
  2. It's billed monthly in advance.
  3. Either of us can end it with one month's written notice.

8. Third-party services

  1. Your store and systems rely on services I don't control, such as Shopify, Klaviyo, couriers and app providers. You hold the accounts and pay their fees.
  2. I'm not responsible for their outages, price rises or changes to how they work. If a change means work needs updating, I'll quote for it or cover it under monthly support.

9. Who owns the work

  1. Once you've paid for a phase in full, you own the custom code and designs I made for you in that phase.
  2. I keep ownership of general tools, techniques and code I've developed independently or reuse across clients. You get a permanent licence to use them as part of your system.
  3. Until you've paid in full, I may withdraw access to the work.

10. Confidentiality and data

  1. I'll keep your business information confidential and only use it to do the work.
  2. Where the work involves your customers' personal data, I act as your "processor". I'll only use it to do what you've asked, keep it secure, not share it, and remove my access when the work ends.
  3. More detail is in my privacy policy at ashgrovesystems.co.uk/privacy.html.

11. Showing my work

I may mention that I've worked with you, and show screenshots of what I built, on my website and in proposals. I'll never show your customers' data. If you'd rather I didn't mention you, just tell me.

12. Liability

  1. I'll do the work with reasonable care and skill.
  2. My total liability to you under any proposal is limited to the amount you've paid me for it.
  3. I'm not liable for indirect losses, such as lost profits, lost sales or lost data.
  4. Nothing in these terms limits liability that can't legally be limited, such as for fraud.

13. Ending the work early

  1. Either of us can end a phase early with written notice.
  2. If you end it, you pay for the work done up to that point. The deposit is non-refundable unless I'm at fault.
  3. If I end it, I'll refund any part of the deposit I haven't earned and hand over the work done so far.

14. General

  1. If there's a conflict, the signed or accepted proposal takes priority over these terms.
  2. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
